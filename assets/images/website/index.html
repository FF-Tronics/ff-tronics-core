Why 47?

Silver is element 47. It is the best electrical conductor of any metal, and every service we offer depends on it.

It carries the signal

Circuits, contacts and connectors rely on silver. Our software, embedded and aerial systems all end up running through it.

It protects

Silver is naturally antimicrobial, which is why it is used in medical devices and equipment.

It lasts

From vehicle electronics to drone avionics, silver keeps a connection reliable under heat, vibration and time.
